# Phase 1 — Brainstorming and Ideation

## Problem
Students often use different tools for tasks, notes, quizzes and study planning.

## Proposed Solution
Create one simple student learning assistant called AI StudyBuddy.

## Main Ideas
- Study progress dashboard
- Study task planner
- Quick quizzes
- Smart notes
- AI-style study assistant
- Team/project information

## Target Users
College and school students who want a simple study organization tool.
